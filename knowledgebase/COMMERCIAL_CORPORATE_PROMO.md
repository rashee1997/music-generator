Commercial and corporate audio does **not** exist for passive artistic indulgence—it is a functional acoustic tool engineered to trigger **cognitive ease, brand trust, buying intent, and emotional validation**.

### 1.1 The "Voice-Over First" Contract (Anti-VO Masking)
In 95% of commercials, YouTube ads, and corporate presentations, a spoken **Voice-Over (VO)** sits on top of the music. 
* **The Problem:** The human voice concentrates its linguistic intelligibility between **`800Hz and 3,500Hz`** (formant frequencies for consonants and vowels). If the background music has heavy synths, distorted guitars, or loud snare drums in this band, the voice becomes unintelligible.
* **The Algorithmic Solution:**
  1. **Static Spectral Carving:** Apply a permanent wide **Bell Notch Filter (`-4dB to -6dB`, `Q = 1.2`) centered at `1,800Hz`** across all chordal instruments (pianos, electric guitars, synth pads).
  2. **Automated Ducking Envelope:** Whenever voice activity is detected, smoothly attenuate the entire music bed by **`-8dB to -14dB`** using an attack of `25ms` and a release of `250ms–350ms`.

---

### 1.2 The "Dopamine Arc" (Instant 3-Second Hook & Micro-Lifts)
Unlike a classical symphony that can take 5 minutes to develop, commercial tracks must obey strict **temporal milestones**:
* **Seconds 0.0 – 3.0 (The Thumb-Stop Hook):** An unmistakable acoustic signature (*a single clean piano note with long delay, a bright acoustic guitar strum, an energetic glockenspiel arpeggio, or a vocal gasp*) that stops the listener from clicking "Skip Ad".
* **Seconds 3.0 – 15.0 (The Building Foundation):** Establish a simple, syncopated, uncluttered groove.
* **Seconds 15.0 – 30.0 (The First "Micro-Lift"):** Introduce a new high-frequency layer (*Glockenspiel, Tambourine, String Staccato, or Handclaps*) to sustain cognitive engagement.
* **Seconds 30.0 – 45.0 (The Climax / "Call to Action"):** Bring in full drums, driving bass, and soaring major chords.
* **Final 3 Seconds (The Logo Sting):** A sharp rhythmic cutoff followed by a clean, isolated **3- or 4-note Brand Audio Logo (Mnemonic)** ringing out into pristine silence.

## 6.0 The Complete Algorithmic Reverb Encyclopedia (DSP Physics)

In algorithmic audio synthesis, you must never rely on black-box plugins. Here is the mathematical architecture of the **4 essential reverb topologies**:

---

### 6.1 Schroeder / Moorer Reverb Topology (The Foundation)
Invented by Manfred Schroeder and extended by James Moorer. Simulates room acoustic density without eating CPU:
* **Architecture:** 4 to 6 **Feedback Comb Filters (FBCF)** in parallel $\rightarrow$ followed by 2 to 3 **All-Pass Filters (APF)** in series.
* **Feedback Comb Filter (Simulates Room Resonance):**
  $$y[n] = x[n] + g \cdot y[n - D]$$
  where delay length $D$ (in samples) is chosen as a **mutually prime number** to prevent flutter echoes, and feedback gain $g = 10^{-3 D / (T_{60} \cdot f_s)}$.
* **All-Pass Filter (Increases Echo Density without Coloration):**
  $$y[n] = -g \cdot x[n] + x[n - D] + g \cdot y[n - D]$$

---

### 6.2 The Shimmer Reverb Engine (The "Magical / Ethereal" Pad Maker)
* **Acoustic Physics:** Popularized by Brian Eno and Daniel Lanois. A portion of the reverb decay tail is **pitch-shifted upward by an exact Octave (+12 semitones / $2 \times$ speed)** and fed back into the reverb tank!
* **Psychoacoustic Effect:** Every note played on an acoustic guitar or piano causes a shimmering, angelic choir-like string pad to swell upward out of nowhere.
* **DSP Algorithm:** 
  1. `Input` $\rightarrow$ `Reverb Tank (3.5s decay)`.
  2. Tap `15%` of the wet reverb output $\rightarrow$ Pitch-shift up by `+12 semitones` via granular delay or phase vocoder.
  3. Lowpass filter the pitch-shifted signal at `4.5kHz` (prevents harshness).
  4. Feed back into the input of the reverb tank with a gain of `0.35`!

---

### 6.3 80s Phil Collins Gated Reverb (The Punch Machine)
* **Acoustic Physics:** Born accidentally in 1979 at Townhouse Studios (Hugh Padgham & Phil Collins). A massive, explosive drum room reverb is **slammed shut by a hard noise gate** before it can naturally decay.
* **DSP Algorithm:**
  1. Feed Snare into a dense Plate Reverb (`decay = 2.5 seconds`).
  2. At `t = 200ms`, drop the gain of the reverb tail from `1.0` to **`0.0001` (`-80dB`) over a sharp `15ms` slope**!
  3. Result: Massive stadium-sized impact that leaves zero mud in the mix!

---

### 6.4 The "Infinite Dune / Cathedral" Reverb Topology
* **Acoustic Physics:** Simulates acoustic waves traveling across open desert canyons or massive stone cathedrals where reflections linger for **6 to 12 seconds**.
* **DSP Algorithm:**
  * Uses an **All-Pass Feedback Delay Network (FDN)** with an orthonormal feedback matrix (e.g., Householder reflection matrix).
  * High-frequency damping filter (`Lowpass @ 2.2kHz` inside the loop) simulates the acoustic absorption of desert sand and cool air, keeping the low-mid frequencies (`300Hz–1kHz`) floating infinitely.

## 7.0 Pure-Python DSP Module: Voice-Over Ducking & Multi-Reverb Engine

Drop this self-contained Python class into your engine. It contains:
1. **`apply_voiceover_ducking()`**: Dynamic anti-masking processor that ducks background music beds automatically when vocal dialog is present.
2. **`SchroederReverbEngine`**: Pure-NumPy algorithmic reverb supporting **Clean Room, 80s Gated Plate, and Shimmer Reverb**:

```python
import numpy as np
from scipy.signal import butter, lfilter

class CommercialAudioAndReverbDSP:
    """
    Pure-Python DSP module for Commercial Persuasion Audio & Spatial Acoustics:
    - Automated Voice-Over (VO) Sidechain Ducking with Lookahead
    - Algorithmic Schroeder/Moorer Reverb Network
    - 80s Gated Plate Reverb
    - Ethereal Shimmer Reverb (Octave-Up Pitch Feedback)
    """
    def __init__(self, sr: int = 44100):
        self.sr = sr

    # =========================================================================
    # PART A: COMMERCIAL VOICE-OVER DUCKING ENGINE
    # =========================================================================
    def apply_voiceover_ducking(
        self,
        music_stereo: np.ndarray,
        voiceover_mono: np.ndarray,
        ducking_db: float = -12.0,
        attack_sec: float = 0.025,
        release_sec: float = 0.280,
        vo_threshold: float = 0.05
    ) -> np.ndarray:
        """
        Dynamically ducks the music bed whenever voiceover activity is detected,
        guaranteeing pristine speech intelligibility for ads and corporate videos.
        """
        N = min(music_stereo.shape[1], len(voiceover_mono))
        music_out = np.copy(music_stereo[:, :N])
        vo = voiceover_mono[:N]
        
        # 1. Compute Smoothed Voice-Over Activity Envelope
        vo_rect = np.abs(vo)
        att_coeff = np.exp(-1.0 / (attack_sec * self.sr))
        rel_coeff = np.exp(-1.0 / (release_sec * self.sr))
        
        envelope = np.zeros(N, dtype=np.float64)
        curr_env = 0.0
        for i in range(N):
            val = vo_rect[i]
            if val > curr_env:
                curr_env = att_coeff * curr_env + (1.0 - att_coeff) * val
            else:
                curr_env = rel_coeff * curr_env + (1.0 - rel_coeff) * val
            envelope[i] = curr_env
            
        # 2. Compute Gain Reduction Curve
        target_gain = 10.0 ** (ducking_db / 20.0)
        gain_curve = np.ones(N, dtype=np.float64)
        mask = envelope > vo_threshold
        # Smooth interpolation into ducked gain
        gain_curve[mask] = target_gain + (1.0 - target_gain) * np.exp(-(envelope[mask] - vo_threshold) * 8.0)
        
        # 3. Apply Spectral VO Carving (-4dB dip at 1.8kHz) on the music during VO
        b_notch, a_notch = butter(2, [1200.0 / (self.sr / 2), 2600.0 / (self.sr / 2)], btype='bandstop')
        music_carved = lfilter(b_notch, a_notch, music_out, axis=1)
        
        final_music = (music_out * 0.4 + music_carved * 0.6) * gain_curve
        return final_music

    # =========================================================================
    # PART B: ALGORITHMIC SCHROEDER & SHIMMER REVERB NETWORK
    # =========================================================================
    def _comb_filter(self, x: np.ndarray, delay_samples: int, feedback: float) -> np.ndarray:
        """Implements a single Feedback Comb Filter (FBCF)."""
        y = np.zeros_like(x)
        for i in range(len(x)):
            if i >= delay_samples:
                y[i] = x[i] + feedback * y[i - delay_samples]
            else:
                y[i] = x[i]
        return y

    def _allpass_filter(self, x: np.ndarray, delay_samples: int, gain: float = 0.7) -> np.ndarray:
        """Implements an All-Pass Filter (APF) for echo density diffusion."""
        y = np.zeros_like(x)
        for i in range(len(x)):
            prev_y = y[i - delay_samples] if i >= delay_samples else 0.0
            prev_x = x[i - delay_samples] if i >= delay_samples else 0.0
            y[i] = -gain * x[i] + prev_x + gain * prev_y
        return y

    def synth_algorithmic_reverb(
        self,
        input_mono: np.ndarray,
        t60_sec: float = 2.5,
        reverb_mode: str = "clean_room",
        wet_mix: float = 0.35
    ) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]:
        """
        Algorithmic Reverb Synthesizer supporting:
        - 'clean_room': Natural Schroeder room acoustics
        - 'gated_80s': Phil Collins hard-cut gated plate
        - 'shimmer': Octave-up pitch-shifted ambient feedback
        """
        N = len(input_mono)
        # Prime sample delay lengths for comb filters to prevent metallic flutter:
        comb_delays = [1116, 1188, 1277, 1356, 1422, 1491]
        allpass_delays = [225, 341, 441]
        
        # Calculate comb feedback from T60 decay time
        combs_out = np.zeros(N, dtype=np.float64)
        for d in comb_delays:
            fb = 10.0 ** (-3.0 * (d / self.sr) / t60_sec)
            combs_out += self._comb_filter(input_mono, d, min(0.92, fb))
            
        combs_out *= (1.0 / len(comb_delays))
        
        # Diffuse through serial all-pass filters
        diffused = combs_out
        for d_ap in allpass_delays:
            diffused = self._allpass_filter(diffused, d_ap, gain=0.65)
            
        # Mode-Specific Processing:
        if reverb_mode == "gated_80s":
            # Phil Collins Gate: Cut tail sharply after 220ms
            gate_len = int(0.22 * self.sr)
            gate_mask = np.ones(N, dtype=np.float64)
            for i in range(gate_len, N):
                gate_mask[i] = np.exp(-(i - gate_len) / (0.015 * self.sr))
            diffused = diffused * gate_mask
            
        elif reverb_mode == "shimmer":
            # Simple granular octave-up pitch-shift (2x speed reading)
            shimmer_layer = np.zeros(N, dtype=np.float64)
            grain_size = int(0.04 * self.sr)
            for g in range(0, N - grain_size * 2, grain_size):
                # Resample half-grain to double speed
                half_chunk = diffused[g : g + grain_size]
                pitched = half_chunk[::2]
                shimmer_layer[g : g + len(pitched)] += pitched * 0.45
            b_lp, a_lp = butter(2, 4000.0 / (self.sr / 2), btype='low')
            shimmer_filtered = lfilter(b_lp, a_lp, shimmer_layer)
            diffused = diffused * 0.7 + shimmer_filtered * 0.5
            
        # Create wide stereo field via decorrelation
        haas_shift = int(0.012 * self.sr)
        out_L = (1.0 - wet_mix) * input_mono + (wet_mix * diffused)
        out_R = (1.0 - wet_mix) * input_mono + (wet_mix * np.roll(diffused, haas_shift))
        
        return np.tanh(out_L), np.tanh(out_R)
```
