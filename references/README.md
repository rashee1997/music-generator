# references/

Instructions and lookup tables for the AI. No history, notes, sources or test results.

- `workflow/` - how a piece is made: brief, direction, score, research, self-check, setup.
- `mixing/` - humanisation, mix rules, mastering, the quality gate.
- `scoring/` - idioms, genres, fusion, culture, video and emotion.
- `instruments-and-vocals/` - instrument, SFZ and vocal technique.
- `spatial/` - acoustic laws, rooms, scenes, immersive staging.
- `sound-design/` - effects and physical sound.
- `data/` - the JSON the engine loads (presets, sound models, styles, vocal data).

Entry points: `SKILL.md` (commands), `AGENTS.md` (rules and the read table), `knowledgebase/README.md` (document ids).
