## Craft pass (director-craft section 1)
1. Hook: a syncopated bansuri riff (D . E . F# A F# A | B A F# E F# D), fifth leap on the off-beat.
2. Arc: mist .3 -> groove .55 -> sunrise .8 -> piper .75 -> crest 1.0 (key +2) -> morning .4.
3. Density: the pipes play alone over their drone for four bars; kit and guitars rest in the mist and the piper section.
4. Lanes: bansuri carries; pipes carry in the piper section; strings answer; kit/bass/tabla pulse; tanpura/pipe drone is the bed.
5. Contrast: a whole-tone raise only after the pipes stop; the full band returns above them.
6. Ending: a quiet morning, Sa alone and the birds. Anti-generic: the pipes' fast reel inside a rock band, one key centre, one pulse.
Harmony plan: pedal in the mist, D-Bm7-A open, key +2 in the crest.
